# WanderMate — Services & Tours Blueprint

Draft · 1 Oct 2026 · editable online at https://claude.ai/code/artifact/11be5d4c-7ba7-4780-95ca-c86886a1caa0

WanderMate becomes India's heritage travel company built on local roots: Kashi is the flagship and proof, and every new region launches only when it has the same depth of local people behind it. This blueprint maps every service and tour before the website is rebuilt, so the site sells a real catalogue rather than a template.

> Prices, margins, policies and new-region details are **placeholders to validate**. Festival dates move every year.

## 1. Positioning

**Promise:** journeys into India's living heritage, designed around how you want to feel and led by people who belong to the place.

**What makes us different**

- **Local roots, not resold packages.** Every destination has a named local lead, like Ritesh and Santosh in Kashi. We do not launch a region on a third-party DMC alone.
- **Living heritage, not monuments.** The weaver, the priest, the cook and the boatman are the product. Monuments are the backdrop.
- **Feeling-led planning.** Travellers start from Awe, Devotion, Curiosity, Stillness or Indulgence, and we build the route around it.
- **One conversation, start to finish.** WhatsApp-first, with a reply within 2 hours and one companion from enquiry to farewell.

**Brand pillars**

| Pillar | What it means in a trip | Proof we already have |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Access | Pre-dawn darshan, front-row Aarti, homes of master weavers | VIP Sugam Darshan, Madanpura silk walk |
| Story | Every stop comes with who, why and since when | Food walk with the story of each vendor |
| Ease | Transfers, stays and tickets handled; no forms or upfront payment to start | Solo "WhatsApp Solo" flow |
| Care | Treated like family, not a booking | 4.9 rating from 121 reviews |

**Name and tagline (proposal):** WanderMate — *Feel the centuries.*

## 3. Service architecture

WanderMate sells five levels of product, from a single two-hour experience to a fully bespoke multi-city journey, all built on the same supporting services. A traveller can enter at any level, and each level is made from the ones below it.

| Level (highest first) | What it is | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5 · Bespoke and festivals | Fully private, any length, plus dated festival specials | Kashi in Private, Dev Deepawali |
| 4 · Circuits | Multi-city private journeys, 3 to 9 nights | The Spiritual Triangle, Rajputana in Private |
| 3 · City journeys | One region, 2 to 5 nights, Private or fixed-date Solo Series | Banaras Unfiltered, Kashi Premium |
| 2 · Day trips | Under 24 hours, for transit and business travellers | Kashi in a Day |
| 1 · Experiences | Single moments, sold alone or added to any trip | Sunrise boat, Silk Walk, Night Food Walk |
| Foundation | Supporting services under every level | Stays, transport, darshan and tickets, guides, photography, traveller care |

Read it bottom up: experiences combine into day trips and city journeys, city journeys chain into circuits, and bespoke trips and festivals take any mix. Supporting services are not sold on their own, except stays and transfers for repeat travellers on request.

## 7. Pricing and tiers

Four tiers, defined by what is included. Price bands are planning assumptions for Kashi, per person on twin share.

| | Solo Series | Classic | Premium | Private |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Who it's for | Young solo, fixed dates | Families on a budget, backpackers | Families wanting comfort | Premium & international |
| Stay | 3-star, twin share (same gender) | Clean budget hotels near the ghats | 3-star premium (Dev Residency or similar) | Heritage or luxury riverside |
| Guide | One guide for the group | Local guide for key sites | Expert guide throughout | Senior expert, private |
| Transport | Shared AC vehicle | AC vehicle, shared on transfers | Private AC vehicle | Private chauffeur |
| Darshan & access | Sugam Darshan included | Standard queues, guided | Sugam Darshan, premium Aarti seats | Sugam Darshan, private rituals, private boat |
| Meals | Breakfast and dinner | Breakfast | Breakfast and dinner | All meals, one private table |
| Photography | Included | Add-on | Add-on | Included |
| Price per person (assumed) | INR 8,999 for 2N/3D | INR 2.5k–4k per night | INR 5k–8k per night | INR 15k+ per night |
| Target gross margin (assumed) | 25% | 18–20% | 22–25% | 25–30% |

**How a price is built:** supplier cost (stay + transport + guide + tickets + meals) + experiences at cost + margin for the tier, rounded to a clean number ending in 99. A "from" price on the website is always the 2-person, low-season price.

**Policies to standardise (proposals)**

- Children: under 5 free without a bed; 5–11 at 50% with no extra bed.
- Payment: no payment to start a conversation; 30% to confirm; balance 7 days before arrival.
- Cancellation: full refund minus 10% up to 21 days out; 50% up to 7 days; non-refundable inside 7 days. Festival tours are non-refundable once confirmed.
- Group discount: 5% from 6 people, 8% from 10.

## 9. Operations and partners

Every trip runs on six supply lines; each needs an owner, a vetted list and a backup before a tour goes on sale.

| Supply line | What we need per region | Minimum before launch | Owner (Kashi) | Quality check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Guides & companions | Storytellers who know the city's character | 3 guides, 1 senior for Private tier | Santosh | Shadowed walk + traveller rating after each trip |
| Stays | Hotels per tier near the core sites | 3 per tier, with negotiated rates | Ritesh | Room inspection + photos on file, re-checked yearly |
| Transport | AC vehicles, drivers who know the lanes | 2 vehicle partners | Ritesh | Vehicle age, driver ID, on-time record |
| Access & tickets | Sugam Darshan, Aarti seating, boat permits, monument tickets | Confirmed process per site | Ritesh | Booked 48 hrs ahead, printed in the booklet |
| Experience partners | Weavers, cooks, musicians, photographers | 2 per experience | Santosh | Fair pay agreed in writing; 10 good runs before a tour uses it |
| Traveller care | WhatsApp support, emergencies, doctor on call | 1 on-call number per region | Founders | Reply within 15 minutes during a trip |

**Standards for every trip:** welcome kit (drink, handwritten city note, printed booklet); day-one briefing and handwritten farewell note; same-gender twin sharing on Solo Series; verified guide IDs and emergency contacts; travel insurance offered; makers paid at source, no commissioned shopping stops, plastic-free water.

**Tools to set up:** one shared trip sheet per booking, a supplier directory with rates, and a post-trip feedback form.

## 10. Customer journey

The enquiry is the moment that matters: everything on the website exists to get a traveller to step 3, and everything after it happens in one WhatsApp thread with one companion.

1. **Discover** — Instagram, Google, referrals, journal
2. **Explore** — feeling finder, journey pages, dates
3. **Enquire** — planner or WhatsApp, reply within 2 hours
4. **Proposal** — day-by-day plan and quote (revise until it fits)
5. **Confirm** — 30% deposit, trip sheet opened
6. **Before the trip** — booklet, reminders, balance 7 days out
7. **On the trip** — companion on call, 15-minute replies
8. **After** — photos, feedback, review, referral (which brings the next traveller back to step 1)

| Step | What the website must do | What the team must do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 Discover | Rank for "Varanasi heritage tour" and similar; journal posts per region; shareable pages | Post on Instagram 4 times a week; ask every happy traveller for a review |
| 2 Explore | Feeling finder, filters, real prices and dates, real photos | Keep tour pages and departures up to date weekly |
| 3 Enquire | Planner that pre-fills a WhatsApp message; enquiry form as backup | Reply within 2 hours, 8am to 10pm |
| 4 Proposal | (later) shareable proposal page per enquiry | Send the plan and quote within 24 hours |
| 5 Confirm | (later) online deposit link | Open the trip sheet; book stays, vehicle and access |
| 6 Before the trip | (later) traveller page with booklet and packing list | Reminder at 7 days and 1 day; collect the balance |
| 7 On the trip | Emergency contacts on every page | Companion on call; daily check-in message |
| 8 After | Review links, photo gallery | Photos within 3 days; feedback form; referral offer |
